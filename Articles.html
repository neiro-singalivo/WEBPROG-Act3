Cyberpunk: Edgerunners Season 2

The wait is over and Season 2 is out now on Netflix. Fans are excited to see how Studio Trigger continues the story of Night City, and whether the new season can match the emotional punch of the first. Expect new faces, old scars, and the same neon-soaked tragedy that made the original unforgettable.

Playing Cyberpunk on Your Browser

You don’t need a powerful PC to visit Night City. Cloud gaming services like GeForce NOW stream the game to your browser, so the heavy lifting happens on remote servers. All you need is a stable internet connection and a decent screen. The trade-off is some input delay, but it’s a great way to try the game on a low-end laptop.

Could D Defeat Adam Smasher?

On paper, it’s no contest. Adam Smasher is a heavily augmented Arasaka cyborg who has almost no human left in him. D has the Sandevistan and plenty of heart, but heart doesn’t stop bullets. In the anime, the fight shows exactly how far apart they are in raw power. D’s only real chance would be a perfect plan, perfect timing, and a lot of luck.

Best Netrunner Builds

Netrunners win fights without leaving cover. Put your attribute points into Intelligence first, since it unlocks more RAM and stronger quickhacks. Pair that with a good Cyberdeck and perks that reduce cooldowns, and you can overload enemies, blind cameras, and wipe out a room before anyone notices you. It’s a stylish, high-skill way to play.

Fan Theory: Lucy Died on the Moon

The finale leaves Lucy’s fate open to interpretation. Did she actually reach the moon, or was it only the dream David gave her in his final moments? Some fans believe the ending is deliberately bittersweet, a wish that can never be confirmed. That ambiguity is exactly why people are still debating it.

Choosing Your Lifepath: Nomad, Streetkid, or Corpo

Your lifepath shapes the opening hours of Cyberpunk 2077 and adds unique dialogue options throughout the game. Nomads bring outdoor grit and a strong connection to the Aldecaldos, Streetkids know Night City’s back alleys, and Corpos understand how Arasaka-style power works from the inside. None of them is wrong, so pick the one that matches the story you want to tell.

Johnny Silverhand vs. V

Two people share one head and want very different things. Johnny wants revenge and a legacy, while V just wants to survive and have more time. Their uneasy partnership is the emotional core of the game, and how you treat Johnny along the way changes how the story ends.

Phantom Liberty and the Dogtown Experience

Phantom Liberty takes you to Dogtown, a lawless district filled with spies, mercenaries, and shifting loyalties. The expansion adds a tense espionage story, new gear, and major improvements to the base game. Many players consider it the best way to experience Cyberpunk 2077.

A Tour of Night City’s Districts

Night City is more than one skyline. Watson is gritty and industrial, Westbrook is glamorous and expensive, Heywood is a mix of everyday life and gang territory, and the Badlands stretch out into the desert beyond the city limits. Each district has its own mood, which makes simply wandering around worth the time.

Cyberpsychosis Explained

Too much chrome and the mind starts to break down. Empathy fades, paranoia grows, and violence takes over. Both the game and Edgerunners treat cyberpsychosis as a slow slide rather than a sudden snap, which makes it far scarier. It is also a reminder that in Night City, every upgrade has a price.
