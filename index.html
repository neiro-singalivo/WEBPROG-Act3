What is Cyberpunk 2077?

Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world action role-playing game from CD Projekt Red, released in December 2020. You play as V, a mercenary trying to make a name in Night City, a huge neon-soaked megacity where corporations run everything and chrome upgrades are part of daily life. After a job goes wrong, V ends up with the digital ghost of rock star Johnny Silverhand stuck in their head, and the two have to figure out how to survive together. The game is played mostly in first person, with choices that shape the story, and it’s based on the Cyberpunk tabletop RPG by Mike Pondsmith. The 2023 expansion, Phantom Liberty, added the Dogtown district and a spy story.

What is Cyberpunk: Edgerunners?

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners is an anime from Studio Trigger that premiered on Netflix in 2022. It’s set in Night City, shortly before the events of the game, and follows David Martinez, a street kid who loses nearly everything and gets an illegal military-grade implant to survive. He joins a crew of edgerunners, mercenaries who take dangerous jobs for money and a shot at a legend. The show is about ambition, found family, and what chasing power costs you, and it introduces cyberpsychosis, where too much chrome makes people lose their humanity. Warning: it’s emotional, and it doesn’t pull its punches.
