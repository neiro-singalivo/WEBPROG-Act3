About the Website

In the Cyberpunk universe, the Blackwall is a massive digital barrier built by NetWatch after the DataKrash. Its job is to quarantine the Old Net and keep dangerous rogue AIs out of the human-controlled Net.

I picked the name because it sounded like a community bulletin board to me. I made the site Cyberpunk themed because Season 2 came out on October 20, during our semester break, so I binge-watched it with the friend who recommended it. I haven’t played the game yet, but I’ve read some of the lore and watched online gameplay.

The site’s look is based on the bright neon purple, yellow, and cyan of Night City and the futuristic feel of the series.
